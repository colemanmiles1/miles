Selected project · Dunewood Yacht Club

Turning outreach into program growth.

As a senior sailing instructor, I saw an opportunity to make the program easier for families to discover and understand. I created a straightforward marketing approach built around email outreach, printed flyers, and consistent parent communication.

3-partStrategy combining email, flyers, and direct parent communication
The challenge

Build awareness and trust.

The sailing program offered a strong experience, but reaching more families required clearer and more consistent communication.

My goal was to strengthen the connection between the program and potential clients without relying on a single marketing channel. The strategy combined direct outreach, visibility in the community, and dependable follow-up with parents.

The strategy

Three coordinated ways to reach families.

01

Email outreach

I managed the club’s email account, responding directly to questions and concerns parents had about the program.

02

Printed flyers

I personally designed the program’s flyers to clearly present the opportunity and expand its visibility among local families.

03

Parent communication

I kept communication consistent and approachable so parents could understand the program and feel confident enrolling their children.

The result

A doubled client base.

Together, the three channels created a clearer path from awareness to enrollment and doubled the program’s client base.

This experience showed me that effective marketing is not only about getting attention. It is also about giving people useful information, building trust, and communicating consistently.
